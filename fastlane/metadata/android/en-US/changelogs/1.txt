Initial release.

• Live MJPEG screen streaming to any browser on local Wi-Fi
• Audio streaming with sample rate and channel selection
• Configurable FPS (5/10/15/24/30/60) and JPEG quality
• Any port from 1 to 65535, with a clear error if one needs root
• Optional PIN or Basic Auth, and self-signed HTTPS
• Settings remembered between sessions
• Manual update check against GitHub
• Rotation/fold aware viewer with auto-reconnect and fullscreen mode
